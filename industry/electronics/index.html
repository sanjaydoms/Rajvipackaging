⚠ Industry Pain Points
What Puts Your Electronics at Risk?
  • ESD (electrostatic discharge) silently destroys PCBs and sensitive components
  • Generic foam inserts don't hold devices securely — parts shift and collide
  • Physical shock during courier handling cracks screens and bends connectors
  • Moisture ingress during shipping causes corrosion and oxidation of contacts
  • High return rates and warranty claims from avoidable packaging failures
