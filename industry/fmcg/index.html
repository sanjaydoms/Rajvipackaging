What's Threatening Your FMCG Shipments?
  • Liquids, food items, and cosmetics packaged in glass break under drops and box drops.
  • Excessive paper and cardboard usage inflates packing times and retail floor garbage volumes.
  • Complex manual packaging slows down fast-moving high-volume fulfillment throughput.
  • Rain during shipping ruins outer corrugated box boxes and print aesthetics.
  • Heavier packaging options increase freight costs across millions of product units.
Industry Overview

Protective Packaging for FMCG

Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG) require packaging that balances robust protection with high-speed operational efficiency and low weight. From preventing breakages in glass jars to securing bulk pallet shipments for distribution centers, our packaging shields products through heavy transit loads.
