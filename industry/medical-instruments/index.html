⚠ Industry Pain Points
What's Threatening Your Medical Shipments?
  • Particulate dust and lint contamination from cardboard boxes compromises sterilisation
  • Vibration during transport causes delicate components and lenses to fall out of alignment
  • Static discharge and impact shocks damage sensitive electronic sensors and displays
  • Inadequate cushioning leads to cracked cases or broken dials on expensive monitors
  • Inefficient packaging processes slow down sterile packing lines in cleanroom environments
Our Solution

EPE Foam Pouches & Protection for Medical Devices

Rajvi Packaging manufactures custom Expanded Polyethylene (EPE) pouches and linings tailored for medical devices. Our clean, lint-free foam provides cushioned cradling that protects surfaces, dampens high-frequency transit vibrations, and ensures products arrive ready for clinic use.
