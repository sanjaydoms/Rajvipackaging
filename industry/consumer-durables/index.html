  • Heavy appliances dropped during forklift loading sustain dents on sheet metal or cracks on plastic trims.

Industry Overview

Protective Packaging for Consumer Durables

Consumer durables like television panels, refrigerators, and washing machines are heavy, high-value, and sensitive to transport drops and vibrations. Edge deformation, cabinet scratches, and screen cracks can lead to costly rejections and brand damage. Engineered foam cushions provide reliable drop safety.
