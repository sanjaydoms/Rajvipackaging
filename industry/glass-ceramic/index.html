What's Threatening Your Glass & Ceramics Shipments?
  • Extreme sensitivity to impact forces causes instant shattering of sanitaryware and glass sheets.
  • Bumps and vibration during transport transmit high energy that shatters packed products inside boxes.
  • Friction between packed components dulls retail finishes, spoiling unboxing looks.
  • Poor packaging creates high retail returns, leading to lost profits and administrative stress.
  • Damp packaging boxes weaken structural boxes, causing stack collapses in damp warehouses.
Industry Overview

Protective Packaging for Glass & Ceramics

Glass and ceramic goods are chemically inert but physically fragile. Even minor shocks or bending pressures generate hair-line fractures that compromise structural integrity. Precision cushioning, vibration isolation, and corner protection are required to eliminate shipping losses.
