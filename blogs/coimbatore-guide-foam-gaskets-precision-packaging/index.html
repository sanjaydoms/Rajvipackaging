Coimbatore's Guide to Foam Gaskets & Precision Packaging

Published: July 2026 · By: Rajvi Packaging Team

Vadodara's diverse manufacturing base—spanning engineering goods, chemicals, and industrial equipment—relies on foam and air bubble packaging to protect a wide range of products during transit. Choosing between the two, or combining them, depends on the specific protection needs of each product line.

Foam vs Air Bubble: When to Use Which

Foam offers structured, shape-specific protection ideal for heavier or irregularly shaped components, while air bubble packaging is lighter, more cost-effective for lower-risk items, and easier to apply at scale for varied product sizes.

Custom Foam for Heavy Engineering Products

Vadodara's engineering goods manufacturers often ship heavier, rigid components that need custom-cut foam to prevent shifting and impact damage, especially important for products with finished or coated surfaces.

Air Bubble Packaging for Cost-Sensitive Volume Shipments

For manufacturers shipping high volumes of smaller or lower-value items, air bubble rolls and pouches offer a practical, budget-friendly protective layer that scales well without significantly increasing per-unit packaging cost.

Combining Both for Layered Protection

Many manufacturers use a combination—foam inserts for structural support and air bubble wrap as an additional cushioning layer—particularly for products that are both heavy and surface-sensitive.

Conclusion
